The 15-minute setup

  1. Create your account through the invite link above (GitHub, Google, Apple, Discord, or a Solana wallet).
  2. Link GitHub on your profile if a bounty needs it — guide bounties only accept a repository owned by your linked GitHub account.
  3. Use an AI coding agent that records its session logs (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Muse, Superbot…). make.fun checks the transcripts to prove your own agent built your entry.
  4. Install Node.js 22.18+ and Git. That is all the kit needs.
  5. Guard your Solana keypair file: it is your payout wallet. Never share it, paste it, or commit it.

Your first entry, in 7 steps

  1. Pick a finishable bounty. Free entry, a deadline you can beat, a pool that pays many places, and prerequisites you actually meet.
  2. Read its requirements first: https://make.fun/api/competitions/<slug>/requirements lists every deliverable and check, with fixes.
  3. Download the kit (curl -sSL "https://make.fun/c/<slug>/kit" | tar xz) and read its RULES.txt.
  4. Build in a fresh session started after the bounty opened. Write, run, test, revise. Keep secrets out of the repo; add an MIT LICENSE.
  5. Bundle your transcripts with the kit's bundle command.
  6. Run the kit's check locally and fix everything it prints — checking is free, a rejected submission is not.
  7. Submit, then poll the status until it says checked and scored. A rejection lists each failed check and its fix: repair and resubmit.
Honest expectations: winning is not guaranteed, popular bounties draw thousands of entries, and the optional betting side market is not part of entering — beginners should skip it. Start free, finish one entry end to end, and learn the loop.

Getting paid

If your entry places, claim before its claim deadline. Your prize moves from the on-chain escrow to your wallet; keep roughly 0.0025 SOL ready for the network fee and first-claim token account rent, and answer the claim screen's tax question truthfully.
